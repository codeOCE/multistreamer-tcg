Everything you need to know about collecting, trading, and building your Castle TCG card collection.

For Viewers & Collectors

Castle TCG is a digital trading card platform built for the streaming community. Viewers earn unique digital cards by supporting their favourite Twitch and Kick creators through subscriptions, channel points, gifted subs, and more.

Every creator has their own exclusive card set. Your collection grows alongside the channels you support, and you can trade cards with other collectors across the entire platform.

Yes. For viewers, Castle TCG is completely free. You earn cards naturally by participating in creator communities - watching streams, spending channel points, subscribing, or receiving gifted subs. There is no pay-to-win mechanic and no card packs to purchase.

Cards drop automatically when you interact with a creator's Twitch or Kick channel. Common ways to earn cards include redeeming channel points, subscribing to a channel, receiving a gifted sub, or having bits cheered in your name.

Each creator controls their own drop events, so the exact triggers vary by channel. Check in with the creator or their Discord to find out what actions reward cards.

Castle TCG has five rarity tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, and Legendary. Rarer cards are harder to obtain and feature enhanced artwork and special visual effects like holographic finishes.

The rarity of a card is determined at the moment it drops and cannot be upgraded or changed after the fact.

You can trade cards with any other Castle TCG user using your unique Castle trade code. Propose a trade by selecting cards from your collection and offering them in exchange for cards from another collector. Both parties must accept before a trade is finalised.

All completed trades are permanent. You can find the trading section in your trading dashboard.

Binders are your personal card organisers within Castle TCG. You can create custom binders to sort cards however you like - by creator, rarity, set, or any system that works for you.

Your collection also includes built-in smart views: All Cards shows everything you own, Favourites shows cards you have starred, and Collectables highlights cards marked as not for trading.

Yes. Castle TCG includes a card battle system where you put your collection to the test. Cards have stats including attack, defence, and HP. You can challenge other collectors and compete on the global leaderboard.

Castle TCG currently integrates with Twitch and Kick. You sign in using your Twitch or Kick account, and cards drop based on your activity on supported creator channels.

Castle TCG digital cards are virtual items and do not carry a guaranteed monetary value. They are meant to be collected, traded between community members, and enjoyed as part of the streaming experience.

Exchanging cards for real money outside the platform is not permitted under the Terms of Service.

Your card collection is tied to your account and remains available as long as your account is active. Collections are not deleted due to inactivity. If an account is terminated for violating the Terms of Service, access to the associated collection may be revoked.

Castle TCG is a web platform that works in any modern browser, including on mobile. There is currently no dedicated native app for iOS or Android, but the site is fully responsive and usable on your phone or tablet.

For Creators & Streamers

Sign in to Castle TCG and complete the creator onboarding. Once set up, you can create a custom card set for your channel, configure drop events tied to subs, channel points, bits, or gifted subs, and start rewarding your community with exclusive digital collectibles.

Your viewers collect and trade cards without needing to download anything. Get started with creator onboarding.

After completing creator onboarding, you can design cards using the Castle TCG Card Studio. Upload your own artwork, set card stats, name your cards, and assign a rarity. Cards you create become part of your channel's exclusive set that only your community can earn.

You retain full creative control over the look and feel of your card set, making every creator's collection unique to their brand.

Yes. As a creator, you configure the drop events for your channel. You decide which actions trigger a card drop - a channel point redemption, a new sub, a gifted sub, or bits cheered. You can adjust these settings at any time from your creator dashboard.

You can get started and create your card set without upfront cost. For details on any creator-tier plans or features, check the creator section during onboarding or reach out through the platform's support channels.
