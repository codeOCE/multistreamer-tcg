Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 24 February 2026  ·  StreamCards

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information StreamCards collects, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Category What We Collect Source
Account Info Twitch user ID, display name, profile picture URL Twitch OAuth
Game Data Card collection, binders, trade history, achievements, leaderboard ranking Your activity on our platform
Technical Data IP address (for security/CSRF), session cookies Your browser/device

We do not collect or store credit card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses. No payment processing is integrated into this platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate your account and deliver game functionality
  • To display your name and stats on public leaderboards (opt-out by not linking your account)
  • To display your name and stats on public leaderboards (opt-out by not linking your account)
  • To detect and prevent fraud, cheating, or abuse
  • To improve the platform and fix bugs

3. Twitch Integration

StreamCards uses Twitch OAuth to authenticate users. When you log in via Twitch, we receive your Twitch user ID, display name, and profile image URL. We do not receive your email address, password, or payment methods from Twitch.

Cards earned through Twitch interactions (subscriptions, channel points, gifted subs) are credited via Twitch EventSub webhooks. No additional personal data is collected through this process beyond what is described above.

4. Cookies & Sessions

We use a single HTTP-only session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels. The session cookie is cleared when you log out.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data only with:

  • Supabase — our database provider, for storing game data (governed by Supabase's Privacy Policy)
  • Cloudflare — our infrastructure provider (governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy)

All providers are required to handle your data securely and only as directed by us.

6. Public Data

The following information may be visible to other users on the platform:

  • Your Twitch display name and profile picture (on the leaderboard)
  • Your leaderboard ranking and card count
  • Cards you offer in public trades

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or financial compliance reasons (e.g. purchase records).

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Children's Privacy

StreamCards is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

10. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including CSRF tokens, HTTP-only session cookies, and encrypted connections (HTTPS). However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy with a new effective date. Significant changes will be communicated through the platform where practicable.
