How Castle TCG Works

Castle TCG lets your viewers collect, trade, and battle with unique digital cards. You decide how they earn them, and we handle the rest.

  • Viewers earn packs through Subs, Bits, or Channel Points.

  • Open packs live on stream with our OBS overlays.

  • Your community builds their binders and competes for the top spot.

Tactical Battles

Let your community compete using their collections. Battles are a great way to increase retention and give cards utility beyond just collecting.

How it works

Viewers challenge each other in chat. Our system simulates the battle based on card stats and posts the results directly to stream via your overlay.

Community Trading

Foster a vibrant economy by allowing viewers to trade cards. Trading encourages community interaction and helps collectors complete their sets.

Why it's worth it

Trading keeps the "TCG" spirit alive. It creates social bonds between your viewers and makes rare cards even more desirable.
