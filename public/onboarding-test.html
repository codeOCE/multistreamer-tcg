How Castle TCG Works

Castle TCG lets your community Collect, Trade & Battle with your unique digital trading cards. You handle the cards, we'll handle the rest!

  • Viewers can earn cards through Subs, Bits, Channel Points & more.

  • Packs can be opened and shown live on stream with our OBS overlays, or by the user on their own Castle account.

  • Your community can collect, trade, battle and flex, all while supporting you.

Why it's worth it

Trading keeps the 'TCG' spirit alive. It creates bonds between viewers, adds value to your cards and lots of hype to big pulls.
